## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-28

First public release of the source code.

### Added

- Financial goals with month-by-month projection of the completion date,
  milestones and plan-vs-actual tracking
- Income sources, expenses, transfers and currency conversions with explicit
  fees; split expenses; recurring templates
- Multi-currency support with user-maintained planning exchange rates
- Safe to Spend with a full breakdown of reserves and upcoming obligations
- What-If scenarios and "Can I buy this?" purchase impact simulation
- Category budgets with spending pace
- Analytics: savings rate, net worth, runway, insights and achievements
- Timeline of expected one-time events
- Home-screen widgets and App Intents (Siri shortcuts)
- Face ID / passcode app lock, hidden balances and app-switcher privacy
- JSON backup export and import
- Local reminders
- English and Russian localization
