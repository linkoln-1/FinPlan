# Support

## Bugs

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/linkoln-1/FinPlan/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
Include the app version or commit, iOS version, device or simulator and exact
steps. Remove real amounts, names and notes from screenshots and logs first.

## Feature requests

Open a [feature request](https://github.com/linkoln-1/FinPlan/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
Describe the problem you are trying to solve before proposing a solution — it
helps keep the app focused on planning rather than growing into a generic
tracker.

## Security issues

Do **not** open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Usage questions

Check the [README](README.md), [docs/FINANCIAL_MODEL.md](docs/FINANCIAL_MODEL.md)
(how numbers are calculated) and [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) first. If the answer
is not there, open an issue with the `question` label.

## What is not covered

FinPlan is a planning tool, not financial advice. The maintainers cannot answer
questions about your personal financial decisions.
